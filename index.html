    Good questions

    Things people ask us first.

    Still wondering about something? Call us. You'll talk to a real person, usually Sam.

    Call (555) 214-0187
    How much does a renovation cost?+

    Bathrooms typically range from $28k to $55k, kitchens from $55k to $120k, and basements from $45k to $90k depending on size and finishes. After your free consultation you'll get a fixed, line-item price, never a vague estimate.

    Do you handle design, or do I need an architect?+

    Our in-house designers handle layouts, 3D renderings and material selections. For structural changes we coordinate with an engineer, all included in a single contract.

    How long will my project take?+

    Most bathrooms take 3 to 5 weeks, kitchens 6 to 10 weeks and basements 6 to 9 weeks. You'll get a day-by-day schedule before we start, and 96% of our projects finish on time.

    Can we live at home during the work?+

    Almost always. We seal off work areas, run air scrubbers to control dust, and for kitchens we can set up a temporary kitchenette so life goes on.

    Do you pull permits?+

    Yes. We handle permits and inspections for every project that needs them, and the costs are included in your quote.

    What areas do you serve?+

    Harlow Valley, Westbrook, Cedar Falls, Lakeshore, North Ridge and communities within roughly 40 km.
